How scoring works

  1. 300 if you answer on the first clue. Each extra clue you reveal makes it worth less: 200, then 100.
  2. Double down (2 per round): twice the points if you're right, and you lose the points if you're wrong.
  3. Hot streak: 3 right in a row earns +50, 4 earns +100, 5 or more +150 each.
  4. Final bet: before question 10 you see its category and stake any part of your score on it.
  5. 50:50 (1 per round) removes two wrong answers.
  6. Get a player right and their card goes in your album. Get it on the first clue and it's a gold card.
